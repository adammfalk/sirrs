## World Rugby Shop

We have partnered with <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/pages/about-us' target='_blank'>World Rugby Shop</a> as a primary kit provider. They provide a wide range of options and have no order minimums for jerseys, etc. Order when you need and have it shipped right to your door.

Please check out their <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/collections/society-of-iowa-rugby-referees' target="_blank">storefront</a> for official Society of Iowa Rugby Referees kit!

Well...most of it...

### Jerseys

Our prior kit colorways were pink and green. With WRS, we've combined them into a <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/collections/society-of-iowa-rugby-referees/products/mens-oxford-reversible-2' target='_blank'>reversible jersey</a>.

We also have a <href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/collections/society-of-iowa-rugby-referees/products/mens-oxford-reversible-3' target='\_blank'>second reversible option</a> of white and orange.

### Shorts

Their shorts options weren't the greatest for the referee crowd, so we recommend sourcing <a href='https://www.steamrollerrugby.com/team-stores/society-of-iowa-rugby-referees/sirr-srs-pocketed-performance-rugby-shorts/' target='_blank'>shorts</a> from our previous supplier, Steamroller Rugby.

### Socks

For some reason, we can't add socks to the store, but any black tall sock will work. Here's a basic <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/collections/rugby-team-uniforms-socks/products/wrs-knee-high-rugby-sock' target='_blank'>example</a>.

### Outer wear, bags, miscellaneous

Please see the main <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/collections/society-of-iowa-rugby-referees' target="_blank">storefront</a> for all other items.

### Whistles

The ACME Thunderer is the standard in rugby referee whistles. It comes in a few sizes and materials, but you can't go wrong with the classic <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/products/thunderer-58-5-referee-whistle-by-acme-whistles-1' target='_blank'>58.5</a>, the <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/products/thunderer-58-referee-whistle-by-acme-whistles-1' target='_blank'>58</a>, or the plastic <a href='https://www.worldrugbyshop.com/products/thunderer-558-referee-whistle-by-acme-whistles-1' target='_blank'>558</a>.
